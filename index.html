The Challenge with Renovation

Renovation should not feel confusing.

Too many renovation projects start with excitement and turn into confusion. Unclear pricing, poor communication, missed expectations, and contractors who are hard to reach can make the process harder than it needs to be.

360 Design & Construction was built around a simpler standard. Listen first. Explain the options clearly. Help clients make confident decisions before the work begins.

Homes and Business Spaces

A Space That Supports Your Way of Life and Work

A good renovation is about more than finishes. It is about quality workmanship, thoughtful fit and finish, and how the space supports your life or work every day.

Whether you are preparing a home as an investment, improving a business space, or bringing a property to the level you envision, Alena brings creative, artistic, and knowledgeable guidance to the decisions that make the finished space work as beautifully as it looks.

For business owners, the space has to do more than look finished. It needs to support your customers, your team, and the way your business actually operates.
