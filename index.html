The Challenge with Renovation

Renovation should not feel confusing.

Too many renovation projects start with excitement and turn into confusion. Unclear pricing, poor communication, missed expectations, and contractors who are hard to reach can make the process harder than it needs to be.

360 Design & Construction was built around a simpler standard. Listen first. Explain the options clearly. Help clients make confident decisions before the work begins.

About 360 Design & Construction

A Woman Owned Company Built on Trust

360 Design & Construction is a woman owned renovation and design company serving Tulsa area homeowners, investors, and business owners.

Led by Alena, the company focuses on practical design guidance, clear communication, and careful attention to the details that make a space feel finished, functional, and personal.
